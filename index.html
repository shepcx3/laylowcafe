Our place

Green booths, high ceilings, good light.

Lay Low opened on the corner of Princess and Sydenham in late 2023. It's a mid-century room with leafy greens, rich browns and a disco ball. We wanted somewhere you can show up any morning and settle in.

  • Vegan & gluten-free friendlyVegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options across the menu.
  • Open every day, 7am–5pmEarly coffee before work, slow brunch on the weekend.
  • Walk in, no reservationsGrab a booth, or take it to go.

Late Night at Lay Low

After five, the disco ball earns its keep.

When the brunch crowd heads home, the café becomes a small venue for live music, art nights and DJ sets with local artists and collectives.

  • Live music Singer-songwriters & jazz
  • Art After Dark Local artists, drinks & music
