Late Night at Lay Low

The café after dark.

Some evenings, after the brunch rush, we dim the lights, start the disco ball and hand the room over to Kingston's musicians, artists and DJs.

See upcoming events

What goes on here.

Every night is different. Here's what usually ends up on the calendar.

01

Live music

Intimate sets from singer-songwriters and jazz players, in a room big enough to feel like a show and small enough to feel personal.

02

Art After Dark

Local artists, makers and pop-ups, with drinks and a soundtrack. Come to browse, shop and meet the people behind the work.

03

DJ nights & collabs

Team-ups with Kingston collectives spinning everything from Afrobeats and amapiano to Afro house.
